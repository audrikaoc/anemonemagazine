Who can submit to Anemone?

We review submissions from creators ages 11 to 25. If you’re over 25, email the editors to ask about an exception. If you’re under 13, have a parent or guardian review our privacy policy before submitting.

How do I find the theme for an upcoming issue?

Our general issues are open-theme. For special editions, look for the prompt on the calls for work page and at the top of the submission form.

When will I hear back?

Responses usually take two to four weeks and can arrive closer to publication during busy periods. If you follow up, include the title and genre of your submission in your email.

How often does Anemone publish?

We publish general issues seasonally and release themed issues throughout the year. Browse the issue archive to see what’s out now.

What is Sankayō?

Sankayō is our education program. Visit Writing Workshops to find workshops or apply to mentor.
