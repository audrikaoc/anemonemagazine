Creative prompts

Try one on your own or bring a draft to a workshop.

Fiction

The last voicemail

Your character gets a voicemail from someone sitting beside them. Write the message, then give your character one minute to decide what to do.

Poetry

Three names for rain

Invent names for three kinds of rain. Give each rain a different mood, then write a poem that lets the sounds show how the speaker feels.

Nonfiction

The object you kept

Pick something ordinary you’ve held on to. Describe it so a stranger could recognize it, then tell the story of the day it became worth keeping.

Visual art

The same window twice

Draw or photograph the same window at two different times. Put the images side by side and let one small change tell the story.
