For contributors

Submit your work.

Anemone welcomes writing, art, and photography from creators ages 11 to 25. For exceptions above age 25, email the editors before submitting.

What you can send

Fiction
Up to three pieces, with a limit of 1,500 words per piece. Flash fiction and short stories are welcome.
Nonfiction
Up to two pieces, with a limit of 1,500 words per piece. Try an essay, memoir, review, or short research piece.
Poetry
Up to three poems.
Art and photography
Up to four pieces. We welcome traditional and digital art. Send a clear image of any physical work. Check the issue form for the requested file format.

First-time submitter kit

Before you send

If you’re under 13, ask a parent or guardian to review the privacy policy and provide consent before submitting.

Simultaneous submissions are welcome. Email us promptly if you need to withdraw a piece.

Publication rights

Contributors retain copyright. Anemone asks for first serial rights and permission to keep published work in its online archive.

Questions?

Ask about eligibility, file formats, or a submission you’ve already sent.
