Poetry

Watercolor

By Alicia Christensen

Red and blue trace the changing feelings of a speaker looking back on a romance.

Read in the original issue View Issue 6 Share

Page 40 in the issue.

Excerpt

Watercolor strokes, connecting constellations, written in the sky; I was the sole reflection in your eye.

Why we picked it

Watch how Christensen returns to the same colors. They carry a different feeling each time, so the poem can show how much the speaker has changed without stopping to explain it.
