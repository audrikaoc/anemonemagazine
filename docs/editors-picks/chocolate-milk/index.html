Chocolate Milk

By GiGi Shallenberger

A visit home from college finds a younger brother, a house full of noise, and a very particular favorite drink.

Read the full piece View Old Leaf, New Leaf Share

Page 5 in the issue.

Excerpt

He revels in noise. It is four in the afternoon.

Why we picked it

Shallenberger lets ordinary details carry the tenderness of this essay. A couch, a video game, and a specific kind of chocolate milk show what it means to pay attention to someone you love.
