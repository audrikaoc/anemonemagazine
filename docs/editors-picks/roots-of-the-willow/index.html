Fiction

Roots of the Willow

By Joseph Lim

A grandfather visits his grandson’s grave and finds the willow he always rests beneath has disappeared.

Read the full piece View Issue 7

Page 18 in the issue.

Excerpt

Each visit, I’d rest beneath its long, curtain-like branches that carried its own grief beside me.

Why we picked it

Lim gives grief a physical place to settle. The missing willow puts a crack in a familiar ritual, and that small change makes room for a much stranger story about memory.
