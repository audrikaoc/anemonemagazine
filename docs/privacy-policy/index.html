Privacy policy.

Anemone Magazine · Effective October 16, 2024

This policy covers the information Anemone Magazine receives when you submit work for publication.

Information we collect

We receive your name, email address, any other details you provide with your submission, and the work itself.

How we use your information

We use submission information to review, publish, and promote work, communicate about revisions and publication, and comply with legal requirements.

Parental consent

We accept submissions from creators ages 11 and older. We require verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information from a creator under 13.

How we protect your information

We take measures to safeguard personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, and destruction. No online transmission or storage method is completely secure.

Your rights and choices

Parents and guardians can request access to, correction of, or deletion of a child’s personal information. They can also withdraw consent. We’ll stop processing the information and delete it unless retention is required for legal or business purposes.

Cookies and tracking

Anemone doesn’t add tracking scripts to this site. The linked services for reading issues and sending submissions have their own privacy policies.

Changes and contact

We’ll post updates to this policy on this page. To ask a privacy question or make a request, email anemonemagazin@gmail.com.
