Submissions

How to Submit Your First Short Story

From picking a magazine to checking your manuscript and keeping track of your submission.

You have a story you want someone to read. Before you send it, choose a magazine that fits the work, prepare the file, and make a record of what you submitted.

Read an issue and pick a call

Read a story from the magazine to get a sense of its range. Then check the theme, deadline, age eligibility, word limit, and number of pieces the call accepts. Anemone’s general issues are open-theme, and themed calls have their own instructions. Browse Editor’s Picks or open the submission calendar to find a call.

Check your story against the guidelines

Use your word processor’s word count. Anemone accepts up to three fiction pieces, with a limit of 1,500 words per piece. Check the instructions in the form for the issue you’ve chosen, then read the final draft aloud and check the spelling of names and places. Read Anemone’s submission guidelines.

Prepare the file

For Anemone, save the story as a DOCX file in 12-point Times New Roman, with the title in bold. Open the saved file and check the first page, page breaks, and last line. If the form asks for a bio or other information, enter it where the form requests it.

Download the Word template

Send it and record the details

Open the form for your issue. Check that the title on the form matches the title in the file and that you uploaded the intended draft. Save any confirmation you receive and keep a short submission log.

Keep track while you wait

Check the magazine’s response guidance before following up. Anemone welcomes simultaneous submissions. If another publication accepts the story and you need to withdraw it, email the editors promptly with the piece title and issue.

For a final check you can tick off as you go, open the first-time submitter kit.
